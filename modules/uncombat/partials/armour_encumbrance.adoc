The listed armour wate is its actual wate in kilograms.
When worn, mundane and artifact body armour contribute an effective load equal to actual wate x Binding.

.Worn Armour Load
****
Effective armour load (kg) = actual armour wate (kg) x listed Binding
****

Add this effective load to the persona’s other carried equipment to determine encumbrance.
Use the full listed Binding, without a Strength reduction.
Do not also add the armour’s actual wate to this load.
Binding 0 armour contributes 0 kg while worn.
This calculation does not change the armour’s physical wate or the persona’s Wate Allowance.

Leather armour weighing 3 kg with Binding 1 contributes 3 kg to encumbrance while worn.
Plate Armour weighing 10 kg with Binding 5 contributes 50 kg while worn.
Armour carried rather than worn contributes only its actual wate and provides no Defence bonus.

Active biomech supports its own wate and uses the xref:hardware:biomech.adoc#_power_state[Biomech Power State] rules instead.
