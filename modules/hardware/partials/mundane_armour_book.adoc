.*Armour Details*
Defence Bonus::
The whole purpose of armour is to decrease one’s chance of being damaged in combat.
The player adds the Defence bonus to her persona’s naked Defence.
While it seems overly apparent, the persona must be wearing the armour to get the Defence bonus.

Binding::
Binding measures how much worn armour limits freedom of movement.
It determines the armour’s effective encumbrance load and can reduce the Dexterity contribution to Defence.
See xref:combat:defence_rating.adoc#_binding[Binding] for the rules.

.*Armour Descriptions*
None::
*Binding*: 0; *Wate*: 0 kg; *Cost*: 0 eps.
No armour, civilian dress or daily attire.
With no armour, Binding and armour load are 0, and the persona can use her full Dexterity contribution to Defence.

Furs/skins::
*Binding*: 3; *Wate*: 2.7 kg; *Cost*: 3 eps.
Classic Neanderthal wear.
They are composed of layers of uncured and unpreserved animal hides.
This armour type is very inexpensive but loses its composition as it rots.

Leather::
*Binding*: 1; *Wate*: 3.0 kg; *Cost*: 5 eps.
Leather is the preserved hide of some unlucky, thick-skinned animal.
Leather armour is light and non-restrictive.

Padded::
*Binding*: 2; *Wate*: 2.0 kg; *Cost*: 4 eps.
Padded armour has 2 layers of leather sandwiching some form of stuffing.
The stuffing is often flammable, and the suit is held together with stitching.

Studded::
*Binding*: 3; *Wate*: 3.8 kg; *Cost*: 15 eps.
Studded armour has 2 layers of leather sandwiching some form of stuffing.
The stuffing is often flammable, and the suit is held together with metal studs.

Ring::
*Binding*: 4; *Wate*: 5.0 kg; *Cost*: 30 eps.
Ring armour has a padded armour base, with large rings covering its exterior.
Ring mail is NOT chain mail.

Scale::
*Binding*: 4; *Wate*: 8.0 kg; *Cost*: 60 eps.
Scalemail has a padded armour base with metal strips (or scales) hung loosely on the outer layer of leather.

Cured hide::
*Binding*: 2; *Wate*: 2.5 kg; *Cost*: 90 eps.
A thick animal hide hardened by curing.
This armour has low Binding.

Plant Fibre::
*Binding*: 1; *Wate*: 2.2 kg; *Cost*: 170 eps.
Plant fibre functions like vegan cured hide and is woven from preserved cellulose.
This armour is light and not very restrictive.

Chain::
*Binding*: 4; *Wate*: 5.9 kg; *Cost*: 80 eps.
A suit composed of many small interlocked rings.
Chain mail is different from ring mail, which is composed of much larger loosely fitted metal rings.

Splint::
*Binding*: 5; *Wate*: 8.0 kg; *Cost*: 75 eps.
Strips of concrete or hardwood affixed to a padded armour base.
Splint mail can be restrictive.

Banded::
*Binding*: 3; *Wate*: 7.0 kg; *Cost*: 160 eps.
Overlapping metal strips fastened to cured hide.

Synthetix::
*Binding*: 1; *Wate*: 1.1 kg; *Cost*: 600 eps.
Any synthetic (Kevlar, fiberglass, plastix) composition arranged in sheet form.
This armour type has a high Defence bonus and is both light and non-restrictive.
Synthetix was previously called plastix and now has a new name to decrease confusion with the artifact armour called plastix.

Plate::
*Binding*: 4; *Wate*: 9.0 kg; *Cost*: 375 eps.
The plate is a solitary metal breastplate covering a suit of padded armour.
Very, very cumbersome.

Plate Armour::
*Binding*: 5; *Wate*: 10.0 kg; *Cost*: 450 eps.
Plate armour covers the persona’s torso and limbs with pieces of interconnected metal.
This armour type is exceptionally cumbersome and highly restrictive.
