.*Strike Weapon Details*
Damage::
Damage indicates how many hit points the weapon removes from the target’s hit points.
The player adds one half her Strength Attribute as a Force Bonus to her damage roll.

Wate::
Self-explanatory.

Reach::
Self-explanatory.

Defence Bonus::
If the persona has proficiency with the weapon, she can add a DEF bonus.
The Defence bonus only applies when the persona is using the weapon in combat.
The player multiplies the DEF Bonus by the persona’s Dexterity score and adds it to her Defence.
