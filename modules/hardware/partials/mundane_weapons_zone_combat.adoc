.*Zone Weapon Details*
Damage::
Damage indicates how many hit points the weapon removes from the target’s hit points.
There are no Strength adjustments for Zone Attack weapons.

Wate::
Self-explanatory.

Radius::
Mundane area of effect weapons are unpredictable.
The player must roll the radius of effect each time one detonates.
An radius of 0 indicates only the target hex is damaged.
The other hex values are the radius of the damage.

Miss Roll::
What dice to use to generate the radius of effect.

Range::
The range of mundane grenades is equal to 1 hex per point of Strength.
A persona with a Strength of 10 could aim at a target 10 hexes away.
