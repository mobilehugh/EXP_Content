= Robot Attack Table

The combat rules in the Roll-Playing System employs an attack matrix called the attack table.
The player refers to the attack table every time she makes an attack roll.
A robot’s attack table is for their baked-in attacks and malfunctioning peripherals.
Robots cannot simply pick up and use any old weapon they find.

For detailed calculations, jump to xref:combat:attack_table_robot.adoc[Robot Attack Table,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

The attack table is composed of 4 roll bonuses spread across 3 attack types.

.*Attack Roll Bonuses*
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_skilled_bonus[Skilled Bonus,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** when using baked-in weapons designed for combat
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_raw_bonus[Raw Bonus,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** robot Raw Bonus is 0
** misused non-weapon peripherals and creative attacks use the Raw Bonus of 0
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_max_roll[Max Roll,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** robots have a Max Roll of none
** the player uses the final value of the kilodie roll
* xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc#_force_bonus[Force Bonus,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** based on the STR of the persona
** the force bonus varies depending on attack type

.*Attack Types*
* xref:combat:attack_types.adoc#_strike_attacks[Striking attacks,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** ramming surfaces, actual striking weapon or a misused peripheral like a hammer or shovel
* xref:combat:attack_types.adoc#_fling_attacks[Fling attacks,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** anything launched or thrown by the robot, a misused pencil dispenser or a bespoke shuriken weapon
* xref:combat:attack_types.adoc#_shoot_attacks[Shooting attacks,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"]
** attacks not dependant on the robot's strength, a misused cutting lazer or a nail gun.

These items create a matrix of arithmetic fun called an attack table.
If the robot attacks with a built-in gun designed as a weapon, the player adds her Skilled Shoot Bonus to her attack roll.
If the robot shoots staples from a stapler designed for non-combat work, the player uses her Raw Bonus of 0.
Both devices may be built into the robot and generated during fabrication; their intended purpose determines which bonus applies.
Ramming follows its own xref:robots:peripherals_offensive.adoc#_ramming[Ramming progression,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
These bonuses are based on the persona's attributes, increase with experience levels.

== Artifact Weapon Use
Robot consciousness is a malfunction and they can only weaponize what is clearly defined within their offensive abilities.
These attacks are generated during robot fabrication when checking for offensive systems.
Another way to gain an attack is through a robotic malfunctation (mutation).
If an evolutation has an attack type they player would use this baked-in attack table.

The robot attack table is not for external artifact weapons that the robot has found laying around.
If a robot has no appropriate articulation they cannot pick up and use a gun, a club, or a grenade.
Full stop.
At Ramming 0, the player must win a Free Will check before her robot persona can attempt to ram a target.

NOTE: Playing a robot is more “alien” than playing an alien.

=== Articulation
Every player will want their robot persona to be able to use artifact weapons that they find laying around.
If a robot has an appropriate articulation that allows them to wield weapons they will use their Raw Bonus when making an attack roll.
While the Raw Bonus is 0, there is no Max Roll when using the Robotic Attack Table.
If there is no articulation for using external tools the persona cannot wield weapons.

== Skilled Attacks
It is possible for a robot to acquire a weapon skill.
A weapon skill can be acquired through a peripheral or a program.
With a weapon skill a robot can use their Skilled Bonus on the attack table with that specific weapon type.
This applies to actual weapons, not to misusing non-weapon peripherals.

== Vocation Attack Table
It is extremely rare for a robot to have a full vocation integrated into their consciousness.
In this edge case the player and referee may choose to generate a vocation attack table.
This is the only way a robot persona is able to employ a fully formed attack table.
For example, the player may use a Mercenary or Nomad Attack Table whan making attack rolls.

The player can use this vocation attack table for their external “found laying around” artifact weapons.
The robotic attack table can be used for baked-in attacks.
Managing the burden of having 2 attack tables is up to the referee and the table of players.
A weapon wielding robot use the the xref:combat:attack_table_anthro.adoc[Anthro Attack Table,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] if the referee and player feel it is appropriate.

Robots can be powerful and being able to use their super charged attributes to shoot things may affect game balance.
