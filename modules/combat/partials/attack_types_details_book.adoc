.Attack Types
* *Strike*
** non-powered; thrusting and striking;
** STR dependent
** Sword, mace, punch, bite
* *Fling*
** non-powered; ranged attacks
** DEX and INT dependent
** Arrow, rock, spittle, spear
* *Shoot*
** powered; ranged attacks
** DEX dependent
** Bullets, lazers, rocket spores
* *Zone*
** multiple targets at once
** DEX and INT dependent
** Grenades, aerosols, farts, bombs
* *Psionic*
** non-physical; invisible; instantaneous
** MND dependent
** Telepathy, ESP, telekinesis
