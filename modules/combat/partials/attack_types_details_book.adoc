.Attack Types
* *Strike*
** contact, non-powered; thrusting and striking; once per unit
** *Attribute*: STR
** *Examples*: Sword, Punch, Bite, Ram
* *Fling*
** ranged, non-powered; hurled objects; once per unit
** *Attributes*: DEX, INT
** *Examples*: Arrow, Rock, Spittle, Dagger
* *Shoot*
** ranged, powered; launched objects; once per unit
** *Attribute*: DEX
** *Examples*: Bullets, Plasma Bolts, Lazer Eyes, Rocket Spores
** includes Sotto and Flotto Attack Types
* *Zone*
** affects everything in area of effect; Fling or Shoot Attack; target a hex; single use only
** *Attributes*: DEX, INT
** *Examples*: Grenades, Aerosols, Farts, Explosions
** includes Grenade and Aerosol Attack Types
* *Psionic*
** non-physical; invisible; instantaneous
** *Attribute*: MND
** *Examples*: Telepathy, ESP, Empathy
