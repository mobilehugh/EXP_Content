== How to Roll
A player could also roll a single 10 sided die 3 times in a row and build the kilodie result 1 roll at a time.
However, users of the Roll-Playing System usually prefer to roll lots of dice.

3 10-sided dice simulate the 1d1000 die roll.
Usually 3 separately coloured 10 sided dice are rolled at once.
For example, roll a green, a yellow and a red 10 sided dice all at once.
In this case we will use the colour free first, second and third die rolls.

* The first die is assigned the hundreds place.
** 900, 800, 700, 600, 500, 400, 300, 200, 100
* The second die is assigned to the tens place.
** 90, 80, 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10
* The third die is assigned to the digits place.
** 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 and 0
* 0 0 0 is 1000 because fun!

Rolling 6 on the first 10 sider, 6 on the second 10 sider, and 4 on the third 10 sider creates a kilodie roll of 664.
One can also roll a 1d10 3 times to get the same result.
