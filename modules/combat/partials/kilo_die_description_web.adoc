== How to Roll
A player could also roll a single ten sided die three times in a row and build the kilodie result one roll at a time.

Three ten-sided dice simulate the 1d1000 die roll.
Usually three separate coloured ten sided dice are rolled at once.
For example, roll a green, a yellow and a red ten sided dice all at once.

* The green die is assigned the hundreds place.
** 900, 800, 700, 600, 500, 400, 300, 200, 100
* The yellow die is assigned to the tens place.
** 90, 80, 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10
* The red die is assigned to the digits place.
** 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 and 0
* 0 0 0 is 1000 because fun!

Rolling six on the green ten sider, six on the yellow ten sider, and four on the red ten sider results in a kilodie roll of 664.
One can also roll a 1d10 three times to get the same result.
However, users of the Roll-Playing System usually prefer to roll lots of dice.
