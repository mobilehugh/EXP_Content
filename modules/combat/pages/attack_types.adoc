= Attack Types

An attack is any action where a persona attempts to injure another persona.
This section focuses on attacks where the persona is trying to injure the target persona physically.
The attack types detailed here include weapons, body parts, and explosions.
The section does not detail attack types like psionics, poisons, or bullying.

Attack types organizes the various methods of killing off personas into a few basic groups.
Attack types separate based on damage mechanism, frequency of attack, and range.
An attack type can include weapons, alien attacks and misused robot peripherals.

Each persona deploys each attack type differently depending on their family and vocation.
These personalized differences are in the xref:combat:attack_table_details.adoc[Attack Table,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] section.

== Strike Attacks
*Non-powered thrusting and striking attacks*

Strike Attacks depend on the persona’s muscle power to inflict damage.
The target must be within the Reach of the Strike Attack.
Strike attacks get 1 attack roll per unit.

An anthro could use a weapon like a sword or an axe.
An alien could use a natural attack like a bite or a talon slash.
A robot could use a malfunctioning peripheral that operates as a club.

=== Strike Weapons
These are non-powered thrusting and striking weapons.
Strike weapons are handheld but can include the hand itself.
Strike weapons include swords, maces, pole-arms, fists, paws, beaks, spears, etc.
They are close combat weapons, and the target must be within the weapon’s Reach and meet any minimum-distance requirement.

=== Reach
[[_range]]Reach is the maximum distance in hexes at which a persona can make a Strike Attack.
Reach applies to Strike Attacks only; Fling and Shoot Attacks use Range.
Count from the attacker’s hex to the target’s hex, excluding the attacker’s hex and including the target’s hex.
If a persona occupies several hexes, count from the nearest occupied hex from which the attacking limb or weapon can strike.

A natural or built-in Strike Attack can hit a target at or within its Reach unless its description says otherwise.
A Gigantic alien with Reach 2 and a Gigantic robot with Reach 2 can both strike a target 2 hexes away.
The same distance is Reach 2 for a Medium anthro using a polearm.
Reach does not grant extra attacks or movement.

==== Weapon Reach
The listed Reach of a weapon assumes a Medium anthro.
Do not add the wielder’s natural Reach to the weapon’s listed Reach.
For other persona sizes or unusual anatomy, the referee determines the weapon’s Reach and any minimum distance.
Weapon length in centimetres is descriptive and does not directly determine Reach in hexes.

Mundane Strike weapons have the following restrictions.
A weapon’s Reach does not automatically permit attacks at every shorter distance.

* Reach 0 weapons require a target in the same hex and can be used while grappling.
* Reach 1 weapons can attack adjacent targets. They can also attack targets in the same hex, but without the Force Bonus. They cannot be used while grappling.
* Reach 2 weapons listed in the mundane Strike weapon table require a target exactly 2 hexes away. They cannot attack targets in the same or an adjacent hex, and cannot be used while grappling.
* Mounted lances have Special Reach. They require a heavy, high-speed mount and strike in 1 direction. They cannot attack targets in the same hex or at distances of 1 or 2 hexes. The referee determines their maximum Reach. A miss requires the mount to reorient for another attack.

These weapon restrictions do not impose a minimum distance on an alien’s natural attack or a robot’s built-in attack.
Contact indicates that a Minute alien or Nano robot must make physical contact with her target before attacking, as described in their size rules.
Humongous aliens and Colossal robots have Reach 3 with a no-attack space of 1 hex.
They cannot strike targets in the same or an adjacent hex, so a target must be 2 or 3 hexes away.
Particular attacks may have special rules.

== Fling Attacks
*Non-powered projectile attacks*

Fling attacks depend on the persona’s dexterity to hit a target, and on the persona’s strength to inflict damage.
The personas may be several hexes apart and still be able to damage each other.
Fling attacks get 1 attack roll per unit.

An anthro would use a weapon like a bow or a sling.
An alien may have evolved natural attack like spitting rocks or flinging quills.
A robot could use a malfunctioning peripheral that throw used batteries.

=== Fling Weapons
These are non-powered projectile weapons.
Fling weapons include rocks, daggers, boomerangs, whips, spitting, arrows, darts, etc.
Fling weapons are not close combat weapons, and there must be at least 1 hex between the persona and her target.

=== Range
Regardless of the range of the fling weapon it is powered by the persona.
Some fling weapon ranges are determined entirely by the persona's strength (STR), while others have specific ranges.
Those fling weapons with specific ranges are still fully powered by the persona.
They cannot be launched without physical effort.

== Shoot Attacks
*Powered projectile attacks*

Shoot Attacks do not require any muscle power from the persona.
The persona uses her intelligence and dexterity to guide the attack
The attack supplies its own energy to reach the target and inflict damage.
The personas can be many hexes apart and still be able to damage each other.
Shoot attacks are limited to once per combat unit.
Examples are bullets, laser bolts, energy beams, and crossbow bolts.

An anthro could use a weapon like a gun or a lazer.
An alien could have evolved lazer eyes or gas propelled bone chunks.
A robot could have an actual baked-in gun or be misusing a construction nail gun.

=== Shoot Weapons
These are powered weapons.
Shoot weapons are the classic guns and lazer weapons from Sciency Fiction and action movies including lasers, crossbows, rifles, fusion guns, death rays, machine pistols, etc.
They are typical artifacts from the Hardware section of EXP.

=== Range
The range of a shoot weapon is completely determined by the weapon.
When the trigger is pulled on the crossbow all range energy is already present.
Any other persona could have done the work priming it.
The key is the lack of physical effort when the attack is made.


== Zone Attacks
*Attacks that affect a zone.*

A Zone Attack affects every persona in its area of effect, regardless of Defence.
A frag grenade delivers explosive damage, while a sleeping gas aerosol may make targets fall asleep.
Grenades and aerosols are common Zone Attack weapons.
Aliens and robots can also have Zone Attacks.

For targeting, attack rolls, areas of effect, and resolving damage or special effects, jump to xref:combat:zone_combat.adoc[Zone Combat,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

=== Range
Zone attack weapons that the persona throws are considered a fling attack.
They would usually use their Raw Bonus unless skilled with the zone attack.
The range of this fling attack is equal the persona's STR.
So a persona with a 9 STR could target a hex up to 9 hexes away.

If the zone attack is delivered by weapon (like a grenade launcher) this is considered a shoot attack.
The range of the shoot attack is determined by the weapon delivering the zone attack (grenade launcher).

== Psionic Attacks
*Mind powered attacks.*

Psionic attacks are listed here to keep EXP grounded in its Sciency Fiction roots.
A Psionic Attack Type arises from an anthro mutation, and alien evolutation or a robot malfunctation that gives unique powers to the persona.
Mutations can be which can be psionic or phenomic.
Phenomic mutations are physical in nature and use existing attack types like Strike, Fling, Shoot, or Zone.
Psionic mutations can be used __instantly__ by the persona, travel across imaginary planes of existence, and are only limited by the mutations description.
