= Zone Combat

Zone combat is for weapons that attack a zone, or an area, instead of a single target.
The best known zone attacks are grenades and bombs.
These attacks wreak their havoc over a bunch of hexes instead of a specific persona target.
In EXP there is a unique zone attack called an aerosol.

Zone attacks instantly impact the entire area of effect and every persona within that zone.
For some attacks this means that every persona is damaged by an explosion and shrapnel.
For other attacks this means every persona must make a saving throw against that effect.
Every target, friend or foe, is automatically impacted by the zone attack.

Zone attacks are not limited to grenades and aerosols.
There are mutations that have zone attacks.
There are robotic peripherals that have zone attacks.
There are alien evolutations that have zone attacks.

By and large the zone attack is activated and everyone in the zone is affected.
There are specific mutations, grenades and aerosols that have unique application of their zone effects.
For example, a grenade that explodes simply damages every persona in the zone of effect.
However, a poison gas aerosol forces every persona in the zone of effect to make a saving throw or go to sleep.
The description found in the mutation, grenade or aerosol takes precedence over the rules listed here.

WARNING: *Zone Attacks impact friend and foe equally.*

== Grenades
A grenade Zone Attack arises from a self-contained object that is thrown or shot towards a target hex.
This object detonates affecting every persona in the zone of effect.
A hand grenade is the most common example from mundane terra.

Most grenades deliver a deadly shockwave along with shrapnel or other special effects.
Artifact grenades can deliver additional effects like fire, gas, or even stranger things.
The grenade affects every persona in the area of effect.
Those persons who are located 1 hex beyond the area of effect are faux realistic lucky.
If the grenade effect is 30 hit points, every persona loses 30 hit points.
If the grenade effect is poison gas, every persona must win a saving roll or take damage.

Grenades are effective and dangerous.
They do not come with warning labels.
Personas cannot safely throw some artifact grenades.
Armour does not absorb grenade damage, however there is artifact armour that designed to thwart grenade attacks.
The grenade area of effect is three-dimensional.

=== Activation
Once the grenade is activated (pin, twist cap, or launcher), the persona is committed to the attack.
The pin is a safety device to keep the grenade from exploding in transport.
Once the persona ‘pulls the pin,’ there is no turning back.
For artifact grenades, a persona may not know how to activate the device.

=== Range
The range of a grenade attack indicates how far away the target hex can be from the user.
Hopefully the range is further away than the zone of effect.
Most grenades are thrown, and the user’s Strength determines the range.
There are grenade launchers that will shoot a grenade much further than it can be thrown.

Range is different from the zone of effect.
The range indicates how far away the target hex can be.
If the user cannot throw a grenade further than its zone of effect they will be hit by it.

.*Grenade Range*
* Thrown: 1 hex per STR
** 12 STR can pick a target hex up to 12 hexes away
** Usually a Fling attack using Raw Bonus
* Launched: determined by weapon
** Launcher may have a 30 hex range
** Usually a Shoot attack using Raw Bonus

In most instances the player is allowed to count the number of hexes for a safe throw.
This means the player would pick a hex where the zone of effect does not harm her persona.
Some tables will only allow a player to count the hexes if they have a grenade skill.
This is an optional, but rather fun, way to use range.

=== Attack Roll
For a grenade to land in the desired target hex the player must win an attack roll.
If they miss, the grenade may land elsewhere.
This would be called a miss, but the grenade still goes off and hits every persona in the zone of effect.

The target hex has a Defence that the player must meet, or beat, to have the grenade land in it.
That arbitrary number is 542.
So the player must make an attack roll 542, or higher, to have the grenade detonate in the target hex.

.*Target Hex Defence*
* Target Hex Defence = 542
** Any attack roll less is a miss

The attack roll is only used if the player is targeting a distant hexagon.
An attack roll does not apply if the persona drops a grenade down a hole or at her own feet.

.*Attack Bonuses*
* *Thrown*
** Fling attack with Raw Bonus
** Skilled Bonus if skilled with grenades
** Force Bonus does not apply to damage.
* *Launched*
** Shoot attack with Raw Bonus
** Skilled Bonus if skilled with weapon

If the persona throws the grenade, the player makes a Fling Attack using her Raw Bonus.
If the persona has Grenade Skill the player uses the Skilled Bonus.
Force Bonus is not applied to the damage of a grenade.
If the persona shoots the grenade out of a grenade launcher, the player makes a Shoot Attack and adds her Raw Bonus to the attack roll.
The persona must have Grenade Launcher skill to use the Skilled Bonus.
An alien with a natural ability zone attack would get to use her Skilled Bonus on the attack roll.
A robot with a baked-in zone attack would use her Skilled Bonus, but a misused peripheral would use her Raw Bonus.

*A failed attack roll becomes a miss and the grenade may not land in the target hex.*

=== Miss
A miss with a grenade can still be a hit.
A miss indicates that the grenade may land elsewhere.
There is a chance the grenade will still even land in the target hex.
As the adage goes, close only counts in horseshoes and hand grenades.

=== Dodging
Players like to have their personas dive for cover if a grenade attack is incoming.
Grenade dodging is movie-style combat fun.
Sturdy protection such as bunkers, rocks, or vehicles can protect a persona from an explosive grenade effect.
This kind of cover would not protect a persona from a gas grenade attack.
The persona must both notice the grenade and be able to react before it detonates.
Concealment offers no protection against explosive grenades.

.*Consideration for Grenade dodging*
. must have movement left in unit.
. must sacrifice attack in unit.
. must win an Awareness roll.

If the requirements are met the referee and player can negotiate the amount of damage reduction, or roll for damage reduction.
The player can roll 1d6 for a 10 to 60% damage reduction.
So if the persona successfully dodges the incoming kinetic attack and rolls 5 on the 1d6 they will only take 50% of the damage.

Dodging a grenade happens in the unit of the grenade attack and cannot be used to replace the xref:combat:move_in_combat.adoc#_diving[diving,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] or xref:combat:move_in_combat.adoc#_jumping[jumping rules,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].


== Aerosols
Aerosols are canisters of death that spray their attack with the push of a button.
The gameplay of aerosols is much more straightforward than grenades, but they are no less dangerous.
Almost all aerosols have their business end clearly marked.
The weapon is generally foolproof, and even the nothing vocation can use them without difficulty.
Weaponized aerosols are always artifacts, and there are no mundane aerosols.

Aerosols do not require an attack roll and do not malfunction.
When activated, the aerosol instantly fills a stream or radius area of effect.
A radius aerosol always reaches the desired target hex within its range.
There is no targeting roll or scatter roll.

There is no saving roll to avoid being enveloped by an aerosol.
Every persona in the area of effect is exposed to its effect.
In most cases kinetic damaging attacks immediately effect every persona in the zone of effect.
There are cases where special attacks force every persona to make a saving throw if they are in the zone of effect.
For example, a poison gas or xref:hardware:aerosols.adoc#_catabolic_dismodulator[Catabolic Dismodulator,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] could force every persona to make a save vs poison or dismodulation.
In the end the description of the aerosol determines if a saving throw is part of the zone effect or not.


*Targets cannot dodge an aerosol.*

=== Streams
The stream aerosol attack is the most common.
The aerosol blasts its weaponized contents straight out the front.
An aerosol with a 2 hex by 9 hex stream area of effect cuts a swath 2 hexes wide and 9 hexes long.
Every target in the jet stream is affected by the aerosol.

=== Blobs
Blob aerosols instantly launch a blob to a target hex within the weapon’s stated range, where it expands into a radius area of effect.
The blob automatically reaches the desired hex.
An aerosol with a 10 hex range, and a 5 hex area of effect, would shoot a blob 10 hexes and then engulf a 10 hex diameter ball of targets (or teammates).
Every persona in the area of effect will be affected by the aerosol, including the user if she is within the radius.

=== Confined Spaces
Aerosols do not exhibit great forces like exploding grenades.
These weapons transform the atmosphere to exert their damage.
Aerosols do not expand beyond the area of effect in a confined space.
A stream aerosol aimed into a tiny room will only affect that tiny room.
