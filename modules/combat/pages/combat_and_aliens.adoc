= Alien Combat

For the most part alien combat aligns with anthro combat and most of the tactical combat rules found here.
The following are some considerations, and frequently offered answers, that arise in alien combat.
There are more similarities than differences.

.*Alien Combat Considerations*
* *Species:*
** the alien represents one member of a species
** different aliens can have different attack tables and mutations
* *Wildlife:*
** are dangerous and unpredictable, but do not attack without reason
** do not have tactics unless described
** do not notice weapons or technology (equals terrain)
** cannot and will not wear armour
* *Hit Points:*
** 0 Hit Points (HPS) is __death__ for an alien
** System Shock, and hence Recovery, does not apply
** Essentially aliens can fight to the death
* *Defence:*
** is naturally determined by skin, hide and dexterity
** descriptive body part types do not affect Defence
** only tool using aliens can wear armour
* *Natural Attacks:*
** these are the evolved attacks of the alien
** the alien is Skilled with natural attacks
** Force Bonus is *not* STR related, and equals Level
* *Weapon Attacks:*
** wildlife cannot see, comprehend or use weapons
** must have at least simple tool usage
** if *no vocation* use Raw Bonus and Level based Force Bonus
** if vocation use Skilled or Raw and STR based Force Bonus
