= Attack Frequency

Attack frequency is the number of attack rolls a player can make per combat unit.
A combat unit represents 2 seconds.
In most cases the player gets to make one attack roll per unit.
This is the expectation unless otherwise stated by the attack, weapon, skill, or ability being used.

== Natural Attacks
Natural attacks and built-in attacks can have an attack frequency greater than one.
These attacks can be Strike, Fling, or Shoot Attacks.
There is no special nomenclature for multiple natural attacks per unit.

=== Aliens
An alien’s attack frequency determines how many natural attacks she can make per unit.
An alien may have multiple tentacles with which to slap, spit several crystal spores, or have multiple natural Shoot Attacks.
The player describes these attacks to suit the alien’s biology.
Aliens automatically use their Skilled Bonus with their natural attacks.

An alien’s natural attack frequency applies only to her natural attacks.
It does not apply to weapons or mutations.
An alien with three natural attacks per unit could bite three times, but would only get one attack roll with a lazer pistol that fires once per unit.
If she uses a Sotto-2 weapon, she gets two attack rolls per unit with that weapon.

The player can use fewer than the alien’s full number of natural attacks.
She can also direct her natural attacks at different targets.
An alien with three natural attacks could attack three different targets in a unit.

For generating an alien’s natural attack frequency, jump to xref:aliens:attack_frequency.adoc[Alien Attack Frequency,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].
The referee can limit excessive natural attacks to preserve game balance and fun.
For an optional way to make multiple attacks more biological, jump to xref:referee_personas:alien_rp.adoc[Alien Referee Personas,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"].

=== Robots
A robot may have multiple pointy things with which to stab, multiple levers that pitch batteries, or multiple built-in Shoot Attacks.
Use the attack frequency specified for the robot’s attack.
A robot uses her Skilled Bonus for built-in attacks and her Raw Bonus for malfunctioning peripherals.

== Skills and Abilities
Some skills and abilities allow multiple attacks per unit.
A mercenary can split her Skilled Strike Attack Bonus amongst several Strike Attacks per unit.
A mercenary can also split her Skilled Fling Attack Bonus between two arrow attacks per unit, although this is rare.
A spie can gain multiple martial arts attacks as she advances in EXPS level.
The xref:vocations:spie.adoc#_martial_arts[Spie Martial Arts,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] rules determine the number and order of these attacks.

== Weapon Attacks

=== Single Attacks
Mundane Strike and Fling weapons normally allow one attack roll per unit.
Shoot weapons also allow one attack roll per unit unless their description specifies otherwise.
A skill or ability can change this frequency as described above.

Weapons with multiple Shoot Attacks have special names and rules.
A semi-automatic weapon is called a Sotto weapon.
A full-automatic weapon is called a Flotto weapon.
Sotto and Flotto are both subtypes of Shoot weapons.
Any persona using such a weapon follows its attack frequency.

=== Sotto Attacks
*Semi-automatic powered projectile attacks*

Semi-automatic weapons allow the player to make multiple attack rolls per unit using her full Skilled Shoot Bonus.
Sotto is a portmanteau of semi and auto.
The weapon allows the player to make 2 or 3 attack rolls with the weapon per unit.
Weapons that attack twice per unit have names like Sotto-2 or Shoot-2.
Weapons that attack thrice per unit have names like Sotto-3 or Shoot-3.
Sotto weapons are usually artifact weapons found in the xref:hardware:guns.adoc[Guns,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] section.

=== Flotto Attacks
*Full-automatic powered projectile attacks.*

Full-automatic weapons allow the player to hose down her target with bullets or lazer bolts.
Flotto is a portmanteau of full and auto.
Flotto attacks allow the player to make 5 Attack Rolls per unit using the persona’s appropriate Shoot Bonus.
A machine gun that releases a hail of bullets with the pull of a trigger is the most iconic Flotto attack.
Flotto weapons are usually artifact weapons found in the xref:hardware:guns.adoc[Guns,window=_blank, rel="noopener noreferrer"] section.

==== Resolving Flotto Attacks
. Player makes 5 attack rolls
. Each successful attack roll is considered a “hit”
. Each hit will deliver 0-3 (1d4 - 1) rounds of damage
. The player rolls damage for each round of damage

The player makes five Attack Rolls each unit when using a Flotto weapon.
The player adds her persona’s appropriate Shoot Bonus to each attack roll.
Each attack roll represents a random number of ammo rounds (bullets, lazer bolts, etc.).
For each successful attack roll anywhere from 0-3 rounds (1d4 - 1) damage the target.
While extremely unlikely, it is possible to win five attack rolls and land zero rounds on the target.
The weapon expends 15 rounds of ammunition per unit, regardless of how many attacks hit.

NOTE: Only a mercenary with flotto skill can “spread” the attack rolls between different targets.

==== Flotto Example
A persona fires her medium-powered full auto lazer pistol at a target.
A medium-powered full auto lazer pistol inflicts 2-20 (2d10) hit points of damage per lazer bolt (round).
The player makes five attack rolls on the target.
Since she lacks lazer pistol skill, she adds her Raw Shoot Bonus to each attack roll.
Three of the attack rolls are successful, two of the attack rolls fail.
The player will then roll 1d4 - 1 three times to see how many lazer bolts (rounds) damage the target.
The player rolls 2, 0, 3 indicating 5 rounds hitting the target.
The target will suffer 10d10 hit points of damage.
The weapon discharges 15 rounds of ammunition regardless.
